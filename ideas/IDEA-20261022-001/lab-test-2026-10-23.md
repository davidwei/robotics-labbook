## Safety gates

The operator retains authority over power and motion. Before connecting:

- Clamp both bases, clear the follower workspace, and keep people and cables outside its swept volume.
- Confirm the follower's correct power supply and steady red motor LEDs; stop for blinking or dark LEDs.
- Keep the power switch or disconnect reachable. Start from a neutral pose with no payload or grasped object.
- Confirm leader and follower calibration IDs are correct and both use the default `use_degrees=true`.
- Never hold, restrain, obstruct, or drive a joint into its limit to manufacture lag.
- Stop on unexpected motion, repeated communications errors, motor overload indications, or loss of operator
  confidence. `Ctrl-C` is the normal software stop; remove motor power if software control is ineffective.

## Run 1 — gentle text-warning preflight

With the operator ready and the workspace clear, power the arms and run:

```bash
set -o pipefail
uv run --extra feetech lerobot-teleoperate \
  --robot.type=so101_follower --robot.port="$FOLLOWER_PORT" --robot.id="$FOLLOWER_ID" \
  --teleop.type=so101_leader --teleop.port="$LEADER_PORT" --teleop.id="$LEADER_ID" \
  --fps=30 --teleop_time_s=30 \
  --catch_up_warning.joint_error_threshold=2 \
  --play_sounds=false \
  2>&1 | tee outputs/lab/so101-catch-up-20261023/teleop-text.log
```

Move one leader joint gently through a small, clear-space motion and return to neutral. The 2-degree threshold
is intentionally sensitive so the warning can be exercised without aggressive motion. Confirm that `slow
down` appears after a genuine visible tracking offset, ordinary control remains smooth, and the cadence summary
does not show a material monitor cost. Do not chase a warning by moving faster.

If no warning occurs during safe motion, record that result and continue to code-level signal injection later;
do not force a hardware stall.

## Success, failure, and evidence

Minimum useful experiment: complete Run 1 and preserve its log plus a brief operator observation, even if no
warning is induced. Runs 2 and 3 are conditional on stable motion and communication.

Success criteria:

- `slow down` is logged when measured arm-joint error crosses the configured threshold.
- `Please slow down` is audible when sounds are enabled; no speech occurs when disabled.
- Recovery below 80% of the threshold rearms a later warning.
- Persistent natural lag produces no more than one request every five seconds and no unbounded speech queue.
- The follower remains responsive, and the timer summary shows no material monitor-related cadence regression.
- Ordinary motion at the default 10-degree threshold does not produce distracting false prompts.

Stop/failure criteria include unexpected follower motion, a control pause or jerk associated with a prompt,
warning spam, speech backlog, communications instability, overload indications, or materially worse cadence.

Preserve after the session:

- exact `git rev-parse HEAD`, full commands/configurations, and start/end times;
- the three console logs that were actually produced;
- a phone-video reference showing leader, follower, and audible prompts if permitted;
- the timer summaries and any observed warning timestamps;
- concise operator observations about usefulness, false prompts, and preferred threshold;
- deviations, missing evidence, aborted phases, and whether the 10-degree default should be retained or tuned.

Create a physical session/run record from this evidence. Do not mark the feature physically validated solely
because the software tests passed or because one warning was heard.

## Fallback and rollback

If speech is unavailable, run with `--play_sounds=false`; text-warning validation is still useful. If the
monitor appears involved in a control issue, stop, preserve the log, and rerun only the known-safe baseline with
`--catch_up_warning.enabled=false`. Do not alter code during the physical run without creating a new commit and
recording the new revision.

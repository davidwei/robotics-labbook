# Share and Publish

Use reviewed lab-book evidence as provenance for downstream outputs.

## Share track
For videos, short posts, demos, and newsletters:
- favor clear storytelling;
- link to relevant experiment/run where practical;
- accurately describe what was demonstrated;
- do not imply general reliability from a single successful demo.

## Publish track
For open-source PRs, releases, technical articles, and reproducible reports:
- identify the supporting experiments/runs;
- ensure experimental code is cleaned and reviewed;
- add appropriate tests and documentation;
- separate validated behavior from known limitations;
- preserve exact repository revisions and relevant configuration/provenance.

A shareable result may be ready long before its code or conclusions are rigorous enough for publication.

The operator launched target-five session `20261031_122309_001c2b60_run_5`. One startup attempt failed before recording when leader motor 6 returned an input-voltage error while reading its homing offset. A retry then recorded and committed episode 0 successfully: 268 dataset rows and 268 telemetry samples, both camera videos 268 frames at 640x480 and 10 FPS (26.8 seconds), and zero telemetry drops. Mid-run visual inspection showed the expected high/wide and gripper-facing views. Seven telemetry samples requested warnings; the largest observed target/follower error was 23.560 degrees at elbow flex on the first sample. No operator task-quality assessment was collected.

The next physical attempt began episode 1 and retained 215 telemetry samples with zero telemetry drops, but failed when camera 0's read thread stopped. It was not committed and did not alter the episode-0 dataset pointer. A subsequent preview also could not open camera 0, supporting an ongoing camera-availability problem after the failure. Session metadata correctly remains at one committed episode out of five. Dataset/video hashes and small evidence are recorded in `runs/RUN-20261031-002.yaml` and `evidence-linux/recording-run5/`; videos remain local. No independent post-failure motor readback was performed during this data inspection.

Kernel evidence resolved the camera failure mechanism: high/wide camera USB path `5-1.2` disconnected at 12:25:47, encountered protocol error `-71` while re-enumerating, disconnected again, and returned as a new device. Its capture node changed from `/dev/video0` to `/dev/video1`; the Innomaker camera remained on `/dev/video2`. A replay attempt using the stale numeric node exited before sending any action. Subsequent read-only arm verification found matching calibration, zero status and torque disabled. Stable `/dev/v4l/by-id/...-video-index0` paths worked in a direct OpenCV test, but the pinned launcher's `lerobot_camera_views.py --indices` parser accepts integers only. The attempted stable-path config therefore failed before preview and was reverted to current capture indices 1 high/wide and 2 gripper. A concurrent 20-second 640x480 stream test returned 489 high/wide and 575 gripper frames with zero failed reads and no new kernel disconnect. This supports recovery, not proof that the physical cable/hub fault cannot recur. Restart the launcher to discard its cached configuration.

After restart with current indices 1 high/wide and 2 gripper, episode 0 replay completed successfully. The replay prepared all 268 recorded reference frames, connected both live cameras and the follower, executed the 268-action episode at dataset rate, and disconnected cleanly with exit status 0.

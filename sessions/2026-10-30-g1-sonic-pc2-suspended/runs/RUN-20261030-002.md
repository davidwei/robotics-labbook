# RUN-20261030-002 — suspended PICO start and stop

G1-29 was fully suspended with both feet clear. After a clean SONIC
initialization and a stable approximately 72 Hz PICO stream, the operator used
the patched full-release A+B+X+Y chord to enter PLANNER and later stop. The
manager logged exactly one OFF-to-PLANNER and one PLANNER-to-OFF transition.
SONIC received both events, ran the IDLE planner policy, then exited normally
after the PICO stop.

The intended three-second interval lasted 22.54 seconds because confirmation and
tool-return latency delayed the stop instruction. The operator observed the robot
trying to step while suspended, with the feet tapping toward the floor, matching
the earlier simulation expectation. CSV telemetry recorded a maximum measured
joint speed of 8.12 rad/s at the left knee, a maximum joint range of 0.821 rad at
right shoulder yaw, and an approximately 1.15 rad base-yaw change over the wider
capture. No sampled motor error/status, LowState/LowCmd CRC failure, recorder
drop, or callback failure occurred.

LowCmd publication stopped and no damping frame or stock-controller command was
captured in the retained 19.84-second post-stop window. The largest measured
post-stop joint change was 0.231 rad at left ankle pitch. This run validates the
live PICO start/stop path only under full suspension. It is not a stale-input,
ground-contact, balance, handover, or untethered validation. Do not repeat until
the longer-than-planned interval and measured knee speed have been reviewed.

See `RUN-20261030-002.yaml` for configuration, measured values and evidence
limitations.

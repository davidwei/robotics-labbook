# RUN-20261030-004 — tethered PICO POSE entry and bilateral leg retraction

G1-29 began full-weight-bearing on both feet with the gantry configured as a
fall catch. After a stable approximately 72 Hz PICO stream and PLANNER standing,
the operator confirmed visual pose alignment and pressed A+X. The manager logged
`PLANNER -> POSE`, and the operator immediately observed both legs retract from
the floor into the air.

Telemetry confirms a rapid bilateral tuck. In the first 0.50 seconds after POSE
entry, the left/right knees changed from approximately 0.249/0.208 rad to
1.960/2.200 rad, while hip pitch changed from -0.085/+0.162 rad to
-1.456/-1.751 rad. Across the 22.98-second POSE interval, the largest leg-joint
range was 2.198 rad at the left knee. POSE output ran at approximately 31--33 Hz,
below the approximately 48--50 Hz previously observed in simulation.

The run does **not** establish that PICO commanded bent feet. Raw XRoboToolkit
body joints and outgoing SMPL packets were not recorded because the manager's
record directory was disabled. SONIC's console trace prints only the pelvis
entry, not tracked knee or ankle values. Incorrect lower-body tracking,
coordinate/joint-order conversion and an out-of-distribution POSE transition
therefore remain competing hypotheses.

After observing the unexpected tuck, the operator intentionally pressed
A+B+X+Y directly from POSE. The resulting `POSE -> OFF` was therefore the correct
terminal-stop transition, not a missed A+X return. The planned elbow cycle and
return through PLANNER were intentionally aborted. The manager exited cleanly,
but SONIC timed out while trying to reset to an uninitialized planner and then
hit a CycloneDDS cleanup assertion (exit 134). Command publication ended;
independent capture continued for 41.80 seconds after the final LowCmd and closed
without CRC failures, recorder drops or callback failures. The operator confirmed
that the robot was secured on the gantry.

This is a failed POSE validation with an effective operator-initiated stop. Do
not repeat POSE on the physical robot until the manager records every outgoing
pose packet and a stationary standing PICO input is inspected offline through
the conversion and policy interface.
